# Zudoku Privacy Policy

**Effective:** August 30, 2026
**Applies to:** Android & iOS

> The published, styled version of this document lives at the Artifact URL shared with the
> Play Console / App Store Connect listing. This file is the plain-text source of record.

Zudoku is a Sudoku puzzle game. It has no account system and sends none of your gameplay to
us — every puzzle, score, and setting lives only on your device. The one exception is
advertising: Zudoku shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device data on
its own to do that. This document explains exactly what happens.

## 1. Information we collect

Zudoku doesn't ask for your name, email, or any account — there's nothing to sign up for.
Everything the app remembers about you is generated by playing and stays on your device, in
the app's private local storage:

| Stored on your device | Leaves your device? | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Puzzle progress & best times | No | Shows your stats on the Leaderboard screen |
| In-progress puzzle (board, notes, timer) | No | Lets you resume a puzzle you started |
| App settings (theme, haptics, highlights) | No | Remembers how you like the app set up |
| Coin balance, unlocked themes, daily reward date | No | Powers the in-app rewards & store |

None of the above is transmitted to us or to any server — we don't operate one. Section 3
covers the one place device data does leave the app: ad requests handled by Google AdMob.

## 2. How it's used

Since the data in Section 1 never reaches us, "how we use it" is really "how the app uses it,
on your phone, to run the game you're playing" — restoring your last puzzle, totalling your
stars, applying your chosen theme, and tracking your coin balance. We have no copy of it, so
we can't use it for anything else, sell it, or hand it to anyone.

## 3. Advertising & AdMob

Zudoku shows a small banner ad during gameplay and an occasional full-screen ad between
puzzles, served by **Google AdMob**. To pick and measure those ads, AdMob's SDK independently
collects information from your device — this collection is done by Google, under Google's own
privacy policy, not ours:

- Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA), unless you've limited it in your
  device settings
- IP address and coarse, IP-derived location (not precise GPS location — Zudoku never requests
  location access)
- Device and app information: model, OS version, language, and ad interaction events
  (impressions, clicks)

Where permitted, AdMob may use this to personalize the ads you see. It's covered in full by
Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy — <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses ad data — <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

## 4. App permissions

Zudoku requests exactly one system permission — `INTERNET` — needed to fetch ads. It does not
request camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive permission,
because it doesn't need them for anything the app does.

## 5. Storage & deletion

All local data is removable, entirely under your control, in two ways:

- **In-app:** open Settings → Reset All Progress to clear every score, unfinished puzzle, and
  preference immediately.
- **Uninstalling:** removing Zudoku deletes its entire local storage — there's no server-side
  copy left behind, because none is ever made.

## 6. Children's privacy

Zudoku is a general-audience puzzle game, not directed at children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. Because ad personalization behaves
differently for child-directed apps, a parent or guardian with a concern about a child's use
of Zudoku is welcome to reach out — see Section 10.

## 7. Your choices

- **Reset your data** any time from Settings → Reset All Progress.
- **Opt out of ad personalization** in your device settings: on Android, Settings → Google →
  Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older versions); on iOS,
  Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Uninstall the app** to remove everything Zudoku has stored, permanently.

## 8. Security

Local game data is kept in your device's standard app-private storage, sandboxed by the
operating system from other apps. Because the data never travels anywhere, there's no
transmission for us to secure and no server-side database that could be breached.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If Zudoku's data practices change — for instance, if a future update adds an account system or
a new SDK — this document will be updated and its effective date revised accordingly.
Meaningful changes will also be reflected in the app's update notes.
