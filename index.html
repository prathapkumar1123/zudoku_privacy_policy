Zudoku

Privacy Policy
Effective August 30, 2026 Applies to Android & iOS Reading time ~5 min

Zudoku is a Sudoku puzzle game. It has no account system and sends none of your gameplay to us — every puzzle, score, and setting lives only on your device. The one exception is advertising: Zudoku shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device data on its own to do that. This page explains exactly what happens, in plain language, section by section.

01

Information we collect

Zudoku doesn't ask for your name, email, or any account — there's nothing to sign up for. Everything the app remembers about you is generated by playing and stays on your device, in the app's private local storage.

Stored on your deviceLeaves your device?What it's for
Puzzle progress & best times No Shows your stats on the Leaderboard screen
In-progress puzzle (board, notes, timer) No Lets you resume a puzzle you started
App settings (theme, haptics, highlights) No Remembers how you like the app set up
Coin balance, unlocked themes, daily reward date No Powers the in-app rewards & store

None of the above is transmitted to us or to any server — we don't operate one. Section 03 covers the one place device data does leave the app: ad requests handled by Google AdMob.

02

How it's used

Since the data in Section 01 never reaches us, "how we use it" is really "how the app uses it, on your phone, to run the game you're playing" — restoring your last puzzle, totalling your stars, applying your chosen theme, and tracking your coin balance. We have no copy of it, so we can't use it for anything else, sell it, or hand it to anyone.

03

Advertising & AdMob

Zudoku shows a small banner ad during gameplay and an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, served by Google AdMob. To pick and measure those ads, AdMob's SDK independently collects information from your device — this collection is done by Google, under Google's own privacy policy, not ours.

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA), unless you've limited it in your device settings
  • IP address and coarse, IP-derived location (not precise GPS location — Zudoku never requests location access)
  • Device and app information: model, OS version, language, and ad interaction events (impressions, clicks)

Where permitted, AdMob may use this to personalize the ads you see. It's covered in full by Google's own policies:

Google Privacy Policy  ·  How Google uses ad data
04

App permissions

Zudoku requests exactly one system permission — INTERNET — needed to fetch ads. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive permission, because it doesn't need them for anything the app does.

05

Storage & deletion

All local data is removable, entirely under your control, in two ways:

  • In-app: open Settings → Reset All Progress to clear every score, unfinished puzzle, and preference immediately.
  • Uninstalling: removing Zudoku deletes its entire local storage — there's no server-side copy left behind, because none is ever made.
06

Children's privacy

Zudoku is a general-audience puzzle game, not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because ad personalization behaves differently for child-directed apps, a parent or guardian with a concern about a child's use of Zudoku is welcome to reach out — see Section 10.

07

Your choices

  • Reset your data any time from Settings → Reset All Progress.
  • Opt out of ad personalization in your device settings: on Android, Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older versions); on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything Zudoku has stored, permanently.
08

Security

Local game data is kept in your device's standard app-private storage, sandboxed by the operating system from other apps. Because the data never travels anywhere, there's no transmission for us to secure and no server-side database that could be breached.

09

Changes to this policy

If Zudoku's data practices change — for instance, if a future update adds an account system or a new SDK — this page will be updated and its effective date revised accordingly. Meaningful changes will also be reflected in the app's update notes.
